# Information-order theory — novelty boundary

Status: **claim-control note**.

## Do not claim as new mathematics

The following ingredients are elementary or have close established counterparts in statistics / decision theory / information theory and should not be sold as novel theorems without a dedicated prior-art audit:

- adding retained side information cannot enlarge a compatible-world fiber;
- deterministic post-processing cannot recover distinctions already collapsed in its input;
- richer observations weakly dominate poorer observations for optimal decision risk when the poorer observation is a deterministic function of the richer one;
- primary-only additive decomposition is non-identifiable without restrictions;
- set-valued / partial identification under incomplete information;
- data-processing / sufficiency-style information ordering.

The executable witnesses are used to make the scientific architecture auditable, not to claim invention of these mathematical facts.

## Candidate methodological novelty

The potentially new contribution is the **scientific observation architecture** obtained by putting three usually separate operations into one compatible-world order:

1. **reference refinement** — retain target-independent side information that can contract compatible measurement states;
2. **record-entry selection** — audit row/support deletion over a separately defined exposure universe;
3. **semantic coarsening** — preserve rich process evidence and unresolved states before any later binary collapse.

The architecture yields concrete design consequences that are easy to violate in sensing systems:

- a reference intended to audit selection must be retained independently of the selection rule;
- a nuisance decomposition should be retained reversibly or set-valued rather than replacing the primary measurement by a residual point estimate;
- an exposure denominator identifies omitted opportunities but not automatically their biological composition;
- downstream classifier quality cannot repair upstream row deletion;
- semantic certainty must not be inherited automatically from an upstream representation change.

## Sister-paper role

- REC supplies the support-selection / missing-row problem and empirical ecological consequence.
- TNOA supplies the process-preserving semantic / partial-identification problem.
- V3 supplies a constructive reference-refinement / measurement-decomposition problem.

The theory paper should therefore present the mathematical results as the **formal glue and audit logic** for these stages, not as isolated mathematical inventions.

## Prior-art audit required before submission

At minimum compare against:

- Blackwell comparison / informativeness of statistical experiments;
- sufficiency and data-processing inequalities;
- missing-data and coarsening-at-random theory;
- sample-selection / selective-label problems;
- partial identification / set-valued inference;
- nuisance-parameter and control/reference measurement methods;
- robust background subtraction / common-mode rejection where the implementation claim is discussed.

The literature audit may change novelty wording, but it should not change the structural definitions or the proved fiber relations.
